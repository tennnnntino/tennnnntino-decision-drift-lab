# User trial protocol V1.4 (not run)

**No trial has taken place. No task time, error rate or satisfaction figure exists.**
This page defines how to measure before anyone states a benefit.

## The task made easier

*After an interrupted working session, decide what can be carried forward from the handoff note
and what must be confirmed with the owner.*

Tool support in V1.4: `python -m ddlab recover <case>` prints a checklist, and browser tab 09
walks through the same case. Both rely on labelled source statements; with unreviewed candidate
labels they demonstrate the mechanism, not a validated result.

## Minimal trial

- **Participants:** at least five people who hand work over in their job (developers or
  operations staff). Record role and experience, nothing else. Written consent; fictional
  material only.
- **Material:** four corpus cases with human-reviewed labels, two for each condition, order
  counterbalanced. Reviewed labels are a precondition: do not run the trial on candidates.
- **Conditions:** (A) conversation and note on paper or in a text editor; (B) the same with the
  checklist or the walkthrough.
- **Task:** list every statement you would act on without asking, and every statement you would
  take back to the owner.
- **Measures:** time to completion (stopwatch, start at first read, stop at "done"); items wrongly
  carried forward; items needlessly escalated; one free-text question on what was confusing.
- **Report:** raw per-participant numbers, medians, the number of participants, and every
  exclusion with its reason. With five participants report no significance test.

## What may be said before a trial

That the checklist exists, what rule it applies and what it cannot see. Not that it saves time,
reduces errors or was validated with users.
